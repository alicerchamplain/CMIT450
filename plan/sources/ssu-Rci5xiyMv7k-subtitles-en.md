Source: Security Service of Ukraine (2021, November 4). *SSU intercepted conversations of FSB hackers
who carried out over 5 000 cyberattacks on State bodies* [Video]. YouTube (removed; archived copy
held locally; page capture at https://web.archive.org/web/20211104114113/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rci5xiyMv7k).
1280×720, 207 s.


## Title cards and speakers

- Title: "SSU intercepted conversations between FSB hackers who carried out more than 5 000
  cyberattacks on state bodies of Ukraine"
- Speakers shown: **Chernykh** (left) and **Sklianko** (right). Caption at 0:08: "Sklianko
  Oleksandr Mykolaiovych, deputy chief of the 4th direction (SCO's, Service of Counterintelligence
  Operations) of FSB RF on the Republic of Crimea and the city of Sevastopol."

## Segment 1 (0:14), card: "FSB intercepted secret documents from one of the Ukrainian agencies"

- Sklianko: Hello!
- Chernykh: Alexander Nikolaevich…
- Sklianko: What are these files about?
- Chernykh: Have you read it?
- Sklianko: And?
- Chernykh: Yes, look, I will tell you briefly how it was… Someone came (*to the head of the
  facility*), they connected a USB flash drive with a crypto container, opened it and started
  printing. And they turned it off immediately. I managed to download it during this time.
  - Footnote: "Crypto container is an encrypted folder that cannot be opened without a password."
